What it does

Dictation in any app

Filler words go, numbers and lists are tidied, and the tone follows the kind of app in front.

Your words

The Dictionary keeps names spelled your way and learns from the words you fix.

Meeting notes

A live transcript with voices told apart. With your own AI provider, summaries with decisions and next steps.

Speech stays on the PC

Parakeet and Whisper run on your computer, on the GPU through Vulkan. An AI provider is asked only if you add one.

Out of the way

A bar, an island or a bubble shows Legato is listening, and stays out of screenshots and screen shares.

No accounts

Word copies of notes for Google Docs, calendars from iCal addresses. Legato never signs in anywhere.

On your phone

Legato for Android puts a small bubble beside the keyboard in any app. Tap it, talk, and the words land in the field. Gboard stays your keyboard.

Your PC's model, or the phone's

Paired, your PC writes up what you say with your Dictionary. Away from it, a speech model on the phone does it with no internet.

In step with your PC

Dictionary, snippets, history, notes and Scratchpad sync over Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth when there's no shared network. No account, no server.

Voice memos

Written up while you talk. Your PC adds a summary, a title and tags once the phone reaches it.

One clipboard

What you copy on the PC lands on the phone, and the other way. Send selected text or a share to the PC, or transform it in place.

How it works

The speech model runs in its own worker process through transcribe.cpp, so a GPU driver crash can't take the app down. The text goes through your Dictionary, snippets and cleanup, and is pasted with the keys that suit the app in front.
