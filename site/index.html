What it does

Everything runs in one small app that starts with Windows and stays out of sight.

{{icon:mic}} Dictation in any app Filler words go, numbers and lists are tidied, and the text is pasted with the keys that suit the app in front. Dictation{{icon:arrow-right}} {{icon:book-a}} Your words, spelled your way The Dictionary keeps names right, learns from the words you fix, and snippets expand a phrase into saved text. {{icon:notebook-pen}}Notes and meetings

Every word of the meeting, tied to the moment it was said

Legato notices a call in Teams, Zoom, Meet, Slack and others and offers to take notes. It records your microphone and the call's audio, writes a live transcript, and tells the voices apart.

  • {{icon:check}}Play it back and follow the word being said. Click any word to jump there.
  • {{icon:check}}Fix a misheard word, or several, and the fix stays on that moment in the recording.
  • {{icon:check}}With your AI provider, a summary with decisions and next steps that catches up after your fixes.
  • {{icon:check}}Copy it into Google Docs or save a Word file. No sign-in anywhere.

{{icon:smartphone}}Legato for Android

Your phone dictates in any app too

A small bubble sits beside the keyboard whenever it's open. Tap it, talk, tap the check, and the words land in the field. Gboard stays your keyboard.

  • {{icon:check}}Paired, your PC writes up what you say with your Dictionary. Away from it, a model on the phone does it with no internet.
  • {{icon:check}}Dictionary, snippets, history, notes and Scratchpad stay in step over Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth when there's no shared network.
  • {{icon:check}}Voice memos written up while you talk, and one clipboard across both.
  • {{icon:check}}No account and no server: the two connect directly and check each other's certificate.
Set up the phone{{icon:arrow-right}}

Yours, on your own computer

Legato has no account and no server of its own. What you say is written up where you said it.

{{icon:cpu}} Speech stays on the PC

Parakeet, Whisper, Canary and other models run on your computer: on the GPU through Vulkan on x64, on the CPU on Arm.

{{icon:plug}} An AI provider only if you add one

Cleanup, transforms and summaries can use Ollama on your PC, or a hosted provider with your own key, kept in Windows Credential Manager.

{{icon:lock}} No sign-ins

Calendars come from secret iCal addresses and notes go to Word files in a folder you pick. Legato never signs in to Google or Microsoft.

Privacy and your data{{icon:arrow-right}}

How a dictation works

The speech model runs in its own worker process through transcribe.cpp, so a GPU driver crash can't take the app down. The text then goes through your Dictionary, snippets and style, and is pasted into the app in front.
