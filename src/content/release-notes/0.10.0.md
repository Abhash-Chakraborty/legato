## Out of your screenshots and screen shares

The island, the bar, the words you're dictating, the bubble and the Notes side panel stay on your screen but no longer show in screenshots, recordings or a screen you share in a call. Turn it off in Settings › Legato bar › Hide from screenshots and screen sharing.

- The island and its Notes pill step aside for remote desktops in full screen, TightVNC included, and stay put for Windows' sign-in prompt and the Snipping Tool.
- Saying "press enter" sends the message even when AI cleanup rewrites the sentence.
