## A cancelled dictation stays in Home

Press Escape by mistake and the words aren't lost. A dictation you cancel shows in Home marked "cancelled, not pasted", ready to copy or transform. If it was cancelled before its words were written, Transcribe brings them back. Cancelled dictations don't count in Insights.

- Closed to the tray, the main window and the Scratchpad stop drawing and give back most of their memory. With the island on, Legato no longer wakes many times a second while it waits.
- Icons in the sidebar, in Settings and in Home's steps move once when you point at them. The Scratchpad shows your voice while it takes a dictation, and its transform arrow travels while a transform runs.
- With Legato for Android 0.2.0, what you copy on the phone reaches the PC by itself, and a dictation cancelled there arrives marked cancelled.
- A voice memo recorded in Legato for Android gets a summary, a title and tags here, as one recorded on the PC does, and they show on the phone after the next sync.
- AI cleanup no longer gives up on a slow answer: a request that hangs is asked again on a fresh connection, and the first answer wins. Long dictations get a little more time.
- Copied files come back on the clipboard after a dictation or a transform.
- The bubble and the Notes side panel land where they should on a second screen.
- A copied screenshot reaches your phone once, not twice.
- Calendar links write spaces as `%20`, which every calendar reads as a space.
- A note's Word copy follows its new name instead of leaving the old file behind.
