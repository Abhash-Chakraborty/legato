- A meeting saved as two notes, because the call dropped for a while, can be joined again: open either note and choose More actions › Merge notes…. The recording, transcript, speakers, tags and tasks become one note, and the summary is written over the whole meeting. Undo puts both back. A call that comes back in the same app within 10 minutes offers Continue notes, which adds to the note that stopped.
- A note's recording is easier to skim. The player shows its bar before you press Play, with the time and line under the pointer, ticks where the summary's topics start, and who speaks where. Back and forward buttons skip 10 seconds, a speed button goes up to 2×, and ← → and Shift+← → move through it. Summary topics have times that play from there. The bar also shows how far it has played, which it didn't before.
- A call that starts while Legato is still saving the last note, or while another note records, is offered notes once that's done. Before, it got no card at all.
- Style works with Settings › Privacy › Remember which app I dictated into turned off. Legato still looks at the app in front while you dictate, so chats stay casual and email stays formal, and it keeps no record of the app afterwards. Before, turning that setting off gave every dictation the same tone.
- With that setting off, transform runs no longer keep the app's name either.
- With Start hidden on, Legato stays in the tray when Windows starts it, including with Work in apps run as administrator on. Before, the window opened at every sign-in.
- With Work in apps run as administrator on, opening Legato from the Start menu or its jump list brings it up again, and `legato --toggle-transcription` reaches it. Before, they did nothing while Legato ran as administrator.
- `legato --help` and the command-line tools print into the terminal you run them from.
- With more than one device paired, the tray's Send clipboard names the one that's connected, which is where the clip goes.
- A Dictionary word can have other forms that are right as written, such as a first name under a full name: Also written as, in the word's editor. Merge on a word's row joins two words into one, with Undo, and Needs review suggests words that look like one, such as a first name and a full name. A misheard surname after a first name in your Dictionary is fixed now, a heard-as form no longer doubles its own word, and a run of ordinary words such as "a bash" is no longer turned into a name that sounds close.
- Correct on the bar after a paste, or Alt+Shift+C, opens what you dictated for editing. Enter puts your fix in place of the paste, fixes it on Home, and offers the words you changed to your Dictionary the way Learn from my edits does.
- Icons move when you point at them, each in its own way: arrows step the way they point, refresh arrows go round, ticks pop and the microphone wobbles. Nothing moves with reduced motion on.
- Your AI model is told more about each dictation: that it was spoken and may have misheard names, which app it goes to, your name, and how your Dictionary spells its words. Cleanup at Medium always takes your Dictionary along now, keeps code and commands as said in a code editor, cleans up a prompt for an AI chat without answering it, and keeps the language you spoke. Polish and your other transforms, and Ask, get the app, your name and the Dictionary too. A follow-up email is signed with your name.
- With the correction pass on, your model suggests names and terms it thinks were misheard but aren't in your Dictionary yet. They wait under Needs review and never change the text on their own.
- Settings › Debug › Check the AI on your fixes runs Cleanup on your newest hand-fixed dictations and says how many fixed words your model spells your way.
- Ask drops an answer in which the model explained its instructions, as transforms already did.
- With an Anthropic key, the instructions go to Claude as instructions rather than at the top of your text.
- A snippet can have more than one trigger phrase: Also triggered by, in its editor. The editor also says when the Dictionary would change a trigger before it's matched, which used to make some snippets never expand, and its test runs the Dictionary first, as a dictation does.
- A Google Meet note no longer lists a shared screen or a shared tab's title among the people in the call; whoever presents is listed by name.
- A note's people say who attended, who was invited but never showed, and who was only mentioned. You're no longer listed among them under your name or your email address, people who declined the invite are left out, and an invite with only an address reads as a name (s.rao becomes S. Rao). Before, everyone named in the conversation looked like they'd been in the meeting.
- A note whose summary failed because the PC was offline, or the AI was slow or busy, gets it written a few minutes later, or when Legato next starts, without waiting for you to press Regenerate. Until then the Notes list says Summary failed instead of Transcript only.
- Summaries say "you" mid-sentence instead of "You", which read like a name.
- Titles the AI gives your notes start with a capital letter, even when the model forgets.
- Style's cleanup example uses a recent dictation short enough to read, not the longest one.
- Home says "on this PC" when today's list also holds dictations from your phone, so the numbers above it add up.
- With every tab closed, the Scratchpad shortcut set to Resume last note opens the note you last wrote in instead of a new blank one.
- Pairing with your phone and Tell speakers apart describe what the app does now.
- A list said as one sentence keeps its whole lead-in: "For the weekend I need oat milk, eggs…" becomes "For the weekend I need:" over the items, instead of losing "I need" or splitting the lead-in into the list.
- Setup's practice email and the examples in Snippets and Style use made-up names instead of real ones.
- Legato runs on Tauri 2.12 and takes the security fixes in the libraries it uses, including the ones phone sync encrypts with.
