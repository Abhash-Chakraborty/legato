## Live and Minimal, easier to tell apart

**Live** now opens to your words as soon as you start talking, with "Listening…" until the first ones arrive. **Minimal** stays a small pill with Cancel, a waveform and Finish. Settings › Legato bar shows a picture of each.

![The Style setting: None, Minimal and Live, each with a small picture of what it shows](/release-notes/0.9.9/bar-style.webp)

- With a model that writes after you stop, Live shows the time, and Settings says why no words appear.
- With a transform after every dictation, the island keeps the same gap on both sides of the waveform.
- A shortcut that shares its keys with another action now says so in Settings › Shortcuts. Before, it just did nothing.
